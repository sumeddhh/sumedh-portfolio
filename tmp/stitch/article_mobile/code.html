Editorial / Tech Landscape

Software Development
in Nepal

Exploring the rapid evolution of the Himalayan tech hub and the architects building its future.

Nepal's digital landscape is undergoing a silent revolution. From the narrow alleys of Kathmandu to the growing outsourcing hubs, the nation is carving out a niche as a high-value software engineering destination.

What was once a landscape dominated by basic web development has matured into a sophisticated ecosystem of AI startups, fintech giants, and global remote-first engineering teams.

"The geographic barrier is dissolving. Nepali developers are no longer just competitors on cost; they are competing on pure architectural excellence."
